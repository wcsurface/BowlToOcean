Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

Welcome to Bowl to Ocean Aquarium Sim ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected through our Android application and associated services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide game progression, state synchronization, and leaderboard functionality during Google Play Closed Testing, we collect:

  • Player Identifier: An anonymized unique account/device ID (`playerId`).
  • Game Save Data: Your current unlocked tank tier, Care Points, diamond balance, owned fish inventory, and bio-load statistics.
  • Technical Metrics: Application version and timestamp data during state sync requests.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Synchronize and restore game progress across sessions.
  • Provide competitive exhibit bio-load rankings on the optional Closed Testing leaderboard.
  • Balance game economy constants (fish costs, Care Point generation rates).

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers. Data stored in our cloud backend (Railway) is strictly used for game functionality.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your game save data is retained for the duration of the Closed Testing program. You may request deletion of your player data at any time by contacting us.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@wcsurface.com.
